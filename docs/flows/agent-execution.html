The rule the shape encodes

No unbounded wait while holding a lock another job needs. The root lock is per-repo; the permit ceiling is per-box. Nesting them made one project's claims kill another project's queue, and the error named the lock (ISS-920: 7 repo_lock_timeout in 30 minutes on codemap, while forge-dev held two of three permits).

Two properties keep it un-nested, and both are load-bearing:

  • The lock band is a lexical block in dispatch::handle. The compiler releases it, not a drop call somebody can move.
  • ClaudeCodeRunner::start touches nothing in the root. Putting root work back inside it re-nests the lock around the permit wait, whatever the block looks like.

The permit wait is bounded too, at one residency window — but a bound is not what makes this safe. For the whole of a bound the lock would still be held and the siblings would still die, just sooner.

Read this before

Changing what dispatch::handle does under the lock, moving anything into or out of ClaudeCodeRunner::start, or touching REPO_LOCK_WAIT / SESSION_PERMIT_WAIT / PRE_SPAWN_BEAT_BUDGET — those three are derived from each other and the build fails if the ordering breaks.
