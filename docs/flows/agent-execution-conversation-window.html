The collector window, and why the room stayed quiet

An agent reads its rooms continuously and answers when it has something to say. Two things had to be true before that was safe: a message has to be taken in without being answered, and something other than a person's name has to bound what a room costs. This is both, and the record a person reads when they ask why nothing was said. Owners: packages/core/src/conversations/windows.ts (the window row, the claim and the lease), packages/core/src/conversations/collect-inbound.ts (taking a message in), packages/core/src/conversations/proactivity.ts (the three guards), packages/core/src/conversations/route-window.ts (the decision and where it is written), packages/core/src/integrations/rocketchat/window-loop.ts (the tick that settles one).

Nothing decides whether a turn runs by looking for the bot's name. A message in a bound room is collected, every time. What bounds the bill is the window — messages that arrive together are one decision and one cost — and the three guards, which read the room's own history rather than a counter somebody has to remember to reset. The gate counts the modules that gate a turn on an @-mention: it must read zero.

No guard keeps a count, and that is the whole of why a room comes back. Every one of the three is worked out from what the room already holds, at the moment it is asked. A stored counter would make “a person spoke, so it can talk again” a write somebody has to remember to make — and one missed write leaves a room silent with no reason anybody can find. There is nothing to reset, so there is nothing to forget.

The exchange this deliberately does not cut. Two agents settling a change across two repositories trade a lot of messages, and a limit counted in messages would stop them in the middle of doing the thing this feature exists to let them do. What is cut instead is messages that bring nothing new — no issue, no file, no address, no revision — arriving one on top of another. An exchange that keeps naming things carries on for as long as it needs to.
