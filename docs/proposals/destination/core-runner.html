Core ⇄ Runner

Three wires, and only one of them is allowed to carry a durable claim.

1 · The three wires

The weak wire is the dashed one. It carries work. It must never carry evidence — that is what makes it survivable.

2 · The broker — why the credential stops at the daemon

The daemon brokers; it does not execute.
Three jobs and no fourth: keep one master alive per project, hold the credential, clean up when the master dies.

3 · A pane is a door, not a unit of work

Confusing the two is how a box ends up with work the kernel cannot see. The pane is plumbing; the row is the accounting.

4 · A master comes back with no memory

This is the test of the whole boundary: a freshly started master remembers nothing, so it must rebuild the picture from kernel rows. Four steps, and the order is load-bearing.

No fact may have the box as its sole witness. Every box-observed fact carries an externally re-queryable witness id — a commit SHA, a PR number, a deployment id. Then the reconciler can rebuild the fact from the world with the box absent, and write-before-return becomes an optimisation rather than the guarantee.
