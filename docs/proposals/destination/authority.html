Authority

Who is allowed to do a thing, how a machine comes to act for a human, and what happens when that permission is taken away.

1 · Everything carries a project. Everything.

"Plausibly wrong" is the failure mode to design against. A cross-project read that throws is an outage; one that returns a valid-looking row from the wrong tenant is a silent correctness failure, and it is the class this fence exists to make unrepresentable.

The fence is enforced where the row is read, not where it is rendered.
A UI that filters by project and a query that does not is one bug away from serving another tenant. The predicate belongs in the data layer, once.

2 · Principals, and the five things authority actually decides

ActWhoWhy it is separated
Admit an Intenta human with write on the projectan agent that can create its own goals has no goal it did not choose
Claim / leasea machine principal holding the projectroutine; the lease is the contention control, not the permission
Answer a Questionan audience, which may be a machinea clarify answered by a peer agent and an escalate answered by a human are one mechanism at two audiences
Force past an unanswered Questiona human, named in the factthe default fires on its own after T2 — forcing early is a human overriding a timer, and it is recorded as one
Waive a floora human, with a stated price and an end conditionthe amnesty is the whole reason the floor means anything; an unpriced one silently retires the gate

These are five different decisions, so they are five different grants. Collapsing them into one admin bit means the only way to let an agent answer a clarify is to let it waive the floor.

5 · Why authority is a fact, not a column

If authority is current-state onlyIf every grant and revocation is a fact
"was this agent allowed to do that, then?" is unanswerableanswerable by reading the grants live at that observed_at
a revocation erases the evidence of what it revokedthe revocation sits beside the grant, both readable
permissions drift and nobody can date the driftthe drift IS the row sequence

Same reason the fact plane exists at all. Authority is one more thing the system is asked about after the fact, so it is stored the way everything else that gets asked about after the fact is stored.
