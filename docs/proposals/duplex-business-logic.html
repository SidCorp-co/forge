Business-logic delta — what duplex changes about how Forge behaves

Not the process lifecycle, not the wire format. What happens to an issue, and what a person can and cannot do while a job is running. Companion to duplex-architecture.html (the design drawn) and duplex-replaces-print.md (the route).

The happy path does not change at all. An issue that enters open, is triaged, is worked by an agent that never asks anything, and closes — that sequence is step-for-step identical before and after. Every change below is on an exception path: a question, a cancel, a human wanting to intervene. Plus chat, which is not the pipeline. If the replacement is judged by the happy path it will look like it did nothing, because on the happy path it does.

What does not change

  • The happy path. Stated again because it is the thing most likely to be misread as a regression in value.
  • How often a job fails. Nothing here touches the 43 % drive-job failure rate.
  • Thinking versus wedged. A session declares waiting versus working; inside working a stuck agent and a busy one remain the same observation. That needs a progress signal with semantics, which is separate and cheaper.
  • Who is told a question is waiting. needs_info is absent from NOTIFY_ON_STATUS. That is a two-line fix scheduled as A1, and it is a prerequisite rather than a part of this.
  • Interventions per issue. The north-star metric is not promised here. Duplex is a capability; inject arguably adds a category of intervention rather than removing one.
