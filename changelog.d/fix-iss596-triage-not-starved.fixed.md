**A filed feedback item's steward triage takes the next run slot its project frees.** It no longer waits behind a backlog of release follow-throughs until a person triages it by hand: of a project's steward runs waiting for a slot, the one due soonest takes it, a triage within 5 minutes of its filing and a follow-through within an hour of its release, so neither starves the other. A triage draft whose item a person routed first is skipped as `triage_taken` on its run, rather than spending a job to write nothing.
