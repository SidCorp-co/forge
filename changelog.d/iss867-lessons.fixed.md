- Lessons are retrieved, never injected wholesale (ISS-867, owner rule prompt-is-a-map). A prompt carries a short lessons map — the slugs of the approved lessons in its scope, inside a hard character budget a test enforces — and the agent reads the ones its work needs from project knowledge by slug or search; an approved lesson's entry is `on_demand` (migration 0577), and a held or retired one is found by no search. A steward turn reads one with `steward_read_lesson` and lists them with `steward_list_lessons`.
- An organisation's on-demand knowledge is named in its projects' guide index, marked `(org)`, and read by their agents through the project's own knowledge door (`origin: org`), with no org access needed; a project entry under the same slug takes its place.
- A feedback item is a correction lessons learn from (ISS-867): a bug or change request triaged onto work is kept in its reporter's words (`feedback_triaged`), and a decline said with a reason as the triager's correction of the report (`feedback_declined`); migration 0578 admits both sources and the item kind `feedback`.
